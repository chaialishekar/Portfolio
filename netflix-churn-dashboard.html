Predictive retention analytics

Churn is predictable weeks in advance. This case study prices it.

Most churn-analysis portfolios stop at a confusion matrix. This one starts from a business requirements document, builds a churn-risk scoring pipeline on 5,000 subscriber records, and ends with a dollar figure retention ops can act on.

What the data showed

Engagement predicts churn. Demographics barely register.

Across 5,000 customer records — demographics, subscription tier, and platform engagement — the model that explains churn best isn't the one built on who a subscriber is. It's built on how they behave. Three engagement features alone explain the overwhelming majority of churn variance, which means the business can flag risk before a cancellation, not after.

Business impact

What a 15% churn reduction is worth
